THE FLOATING WATER MIRAGE

Taj Lake Palace

"A pristine white marble sanctuary floating majestically upon the azure waters of Lake Pichola."

Taj Lake Palace is a legendary love poem carved in white marble. Built in 1746 by Maharana Jagat Singh II as a summer pleasure palace, it represents the absolute zenith of Mewari luxury. Surrounded by calm lake waters, it offers a secluded royal sanctuary for the world's most elite travelers.
